The Problems

Issues in
SG

Before running, I listened. I talked to students, club leaders, and faculty. Three critical financial issues kept coming up.

01

Lack of Budget Transparency

Students fund this government through fees, but most have no idea where that money goes.

"...tf? 136k?"

Yeah, it is — according to the April 30, 2025 Senate Meeting Agenda. This has been a recurring issue since 2023, and it ends with me.

📰 Editorial: Student government lacks transparency about budgetThe Express

02

Club Funding Delays

Clubs experience chronic delays in receiving approved funding, forcing cancelled events and out-of-pocket costs.

"Oh, I'm planning on transferring to Berkeley or LA..."

Around 60% of us intend to transfer, and clubs can be a huge boost to our applications. When they can't operate, everyone loses.

📰 LPCSG stalls club funding with communication delaysThe Express

03

Low Student Awareness & Engagement

Just because LPC is a commuter school doesn't mean it has to feel like one.

"Bro, I only drive here cuz attendance is graded"

Last year, less than 12% of the student body voted. But this campus belongs to all 8,000 of us. Not just those who vote.

The Plan

My
Vision

Policy without creativity is just process. Here's what I'll do on day one — and what I'll build toward.

Core Commitments

01 — Radical Budget Transparency

Publish a plain-English budget summary every semester. Build a public spending dashboard any student can access — no jargon, no gatekeeping.

  • Semester budget summaries in accessible language
  • Online spending tracker updated monthly
  • Financial reports at open student town halls
  • All agendas and minutes searchable online

02 — Fix Club Funding

Streamline the reimbursement process. Set clear timelines. Build a simple tracker so clubs always know where their request stands.

  • Map and document the current process
  • Propose maximum processing time standards
  • Simple status tracker for all requests
  • Monthly office hours for club treasurers

03 — Student Voice in Every Budget

No major financial decision without students having a chance to weigh in — before the vote, not after.

  • Pre-budget feedback sessions each semester
  • Anonymous suggestion channel on this site
  • Student feedback summaries in all proposals
  • Published outcomes showing how input shaped decisions

Ideas in Action

🔬 Clubs as Problem-Solvers

Introduce real campus case studies into club projects. Clubs that solve genuine problems earn recognition, awards, and verified volunteer hours — turning extracurriculars into résumé-building experience.

📊 Data-Driven Student Government

Partner with the Data Science and Journalism clubs to run semesterly surveys and data analyses. Students get research experience; Student Government gets the data it needs to spend wisely.

💡 Club Fundraising that Meets Real Needs

Match genuine student needs to clubs that can meet them through fundraising.

  • 📸 Film/Photography Club: LinkedIn headshots as a fundraiser
  • ✂️ Art & Design Club: suit tailoring for interview prep
  • 👑 Clash Royale Club: it's gonna happen someday ig...
